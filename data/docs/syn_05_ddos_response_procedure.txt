DDOS RESPONSE PROCEDURE (SYNTHETIC)
===================================

SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT: This document is fictional and was created for the APA Secure Incident Knowledge Assistant project. The Aurelian Institute of Technology (AIT) does not exist.

1. DOCUMENT CONTROL

This procedure has document ID AIT-IR-DD-005 and is at version 1.2.

2. DETECTION THRESHOLDS

Normal traffic averages 2.4 Gbps. A warning alert is raised at 6 Gbps sustained for 5 minutes. The critical threshold is 12 Gbps.

3. MITIGATION

Tier 2 enables WAF Under Attack mode when the warning threshold is reached. The ISP scrubbing service from NorthLink is activated by calling the 24x7 hotline at extension 7010.

DNS record TTLs are lowered to 300 seconds in preparation for traffic redirection. The login portal is limited to 100 requests per second per client IP address. Geo-blocking requires CISO approval and may last at most 24 hours.

4. PRIORITIES AND COMMUNICATION

Service restoration priority is the learning portal first, then email, then the library system. The status page is updated within 20 minutes of a confirmed attack.

5. ANALYSIS AND ESCALATION

Analysts capture 15-minute packet samples for later analysis. The Incident Manager is engaged when an outage exceeds 30 minutes.

6. RESTRICTIONS AND REPORTING

Retaliation against attacking systems, known as hack-back, is prohibited. An after-action report is due within 5 business days.

7. ROLES

The Network Team operates the edge routers and requests traffic scrubbing from the ISP. Tier 2 analysts tune the web application firewall during an attack. The Communications Office publishes status page updates, and the Incident Manager decides on service restoration priorities when several services are affected.

8. DEFINITIONS

A distributed denial-of-service attack tries to make a service unavailable by overwhelming it with traffic from many sources. Scrubbing means filtering attack traffic away from legitimate traffic before it reaches the campus network. A web application firewall filters web requests and can challenge suspicious clients. Geo-blocking denies traffic based on the country of the source address.
