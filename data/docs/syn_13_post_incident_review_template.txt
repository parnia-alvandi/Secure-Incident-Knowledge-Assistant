AIT POST-INCIDENT REVIEW TEMPLATE
=================================

Document ID: AIT-IR-TPL-013 | Version: 1.2 | Owner: SOC Manager | Effective date: 12 February 2026


1. Purpose
----------

A post-incident review (PIR) turns an incident into lasting improvement. This template standardises how the Aurelian Institute of Technology (AIT) records what happened, why it happened and what will change.


2. Ground Rules
---------------

- Blameless: the review examines processes, tools and decisions, never the personal fault of individuals.
- A PIR is mandatory for Severity 1 and Severity 2 incidents. For Severity 1 the review meeting is held within 5 business days of incident closure, and for Severity 2 within 10 business days.
- The meeting is led by a facilitator who was not the Incident Commander for the incident.
- Required attendees: the Incident Commander, the responders involved, the owner of each affected system and a representative of the Help Desk.

3. Template Sections
--------------------

Section 1 - Summary: two to three sentences describing the incident, its severity and its outcome, written for a non-technical reader.

Section 2 - Timeline: key events in UTC, including first malicious activity, detection, declaration, containment, eradication and recovery.

Section 3 - Detection: how the incident was found and whether an existing control should have found it sooner.

Section 4 - Impact: affected systems, number of users, data involved, downtime in hours and estimated cost.

Section 5 - Root Cause: the underlying cause, found by asking why repeatedly until a process or control gap is identified; the 5 Whys method is recommended.

Section 6 - What Went Well: practices and tools that worked and should be kept.

Section 7 - What Went Poorly: delays, gaps and surprises.

Section 8 - Action Items: each item has an owner, a due date and a priority.


4. Metrics
----------

- MTTD (mean time to detect): time from first malicious activity to detection.
- MTTC (mean time to contain): time from detection to containment.
- MTTR (mean time to recover): time from detection to full restoration of service.

5. Action Items and Publication
-------------------------------

Action items must be due within 30 days unless the SOC Manager approves a longer period. Overdue items are reported at the monthly security review.

The final report is published within 15 business days of the meeting. It is stored in the restricted incident repository and retained for 7 years.
