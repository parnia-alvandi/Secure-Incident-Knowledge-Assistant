AIT NETWORK SEGMENTATION STANDARD
=================================

Document ID: AIT-SEC-STD-018 | Version: 1.2 | Owner: Network Security Manager | Effective date: 20 September 2026


1. Purpose
----------

This standard defines the network zones of the Aurelian Institute of Technology (AIT) and the rules for traffic between them, so that a compromise in one zone does not spread freely to others.


2. Zones
--------

- DMZ: internet-facing web, mail gateway and reverse proxy servers.
- Corporate: staff workstations and office printers.
- Student: student devices and campus Wi-Fi.
- Research: research clusters and lab equipment.
- Server: production application and database servers, including the student records system.
- Management: hypervisors, backup servers and network device management interfaces.
- Building: building control and physical access systems.

3. Traffic Rules
----------------

- Traffic between zones is denied by default; only flows listed in the firewall rule base are allowed.
- DMZ servers may not initiate connections into the Corporate or Student zones.
- The Student zone has no direct access to the Server zone.
- The Management zone is reachable only from the jump hosts, and jump host logins require multi-factor authentication.
- Servers may initiate outbound internet connections only to destinations on an approved allow list.
- The Building zone has no route to the internet.

4. Rule Governance
------------------

Every firewall rule has a named owner, a written justification and an expiry date. Temporary rules last at most 30 days. Research zone exceptions last at most 12 months.

All rules are reviewed every 6 months, and rules that are not re-approved at the review are removed.


5. Emergency Isolation
----------------------

During an incident the SOC may move a host to the quarantine VLAN, VLAN 999, without prior approval. The SOC informs the Incident Commander within 30 minutes of the move.

VLAN 999 permits traffic only to the SOC investigation host.


6. Definitions
--------------

- Zone: a group of systems with a similar level of trust and function, separated from other groups by a firewall.
- Jump host: a hardened server that is the only path administrators may use to reach the Management zone.
- Firewall rule base: the complete list of permitted flows between zones, maintained by the Network Security Team.
- Quarantine VLAN: an isolated network segment used to contain a host that is suspected to be compromised.

7. Roles and Exceptions
-----------------------

The Network Security Manager owns the firewall rule base. System owners request new rules and re-approve their own rules at each review. The SOC applies emergency isolation but does not change the rule base.

Requests for exceptions to this standard are sent to the Network Security Manager and need the approval of the CISO. Every exception is recorded together with its expiry date.

Emergency isolation is also used by the Web Server Compromise and Webshell Response Playbook.
